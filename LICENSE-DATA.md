## Sources that carry their own terms

Some inputs to this study are not produced by it and keep their own terms rather than the CC BY
4.0 line above. Each is named here with its source and its own licence; this repository's
CC BY 4.0 grant does not extend to them.

- The blank forms checked by the tool under study are official US government forms (a US
  government work under 17 U.S.C. 105, no copyright) and an official French administrative form
  released under its administration's open terms. Exact form identifiers and their own source
  pages are recorded next to the fixtures that use them.
- The NAF dataset (github.com/herobd/NAF_dataset), used for the archival prevalence measurement in
  `naf/`, is released under the Community Data License Agreement Permissive 1.0. Derived per-field
  measurements this study computes from NAF images are released here under CC BY 4.0 with
  attribution to the NAF dataset and its original publication; NAF's own images and annotation
  files, fetched from upstream by this study's scripts, keep the CDLA-Permissive-1.0 terms and are
  not redistributed as data files in this repository.
- Any capture image of a real, physically printed and hand-marked form used in this study depicts
  fictional identities only, with identifying metadata (EXIF, GPS) stripped and verified stripped
  before release, and is released under CC BY 4.0 like the rest of this study's derived data.

A full accounting of every third-party input, its source, its retrieval date and its own licence
lives in the preregistration and the environment record once collection is complete.
